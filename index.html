About Carta Lotto

Carta Lotto is an independent informational website that publishes dated Carta Ramalan 4D number charts. GDL Perdana is published daily; Ramalan 4D MTP and SGP are published on their scheduled days (Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday). Every chart is kept on its own permanent, dated page.

Carta Lotto is not an official lottery operator, does not sell tickets, and does not guarantee any future outcome. Highlighted positions within a chart are visual markers on the published layout only.

Important: Carta Lotto does not guarantee winning numbers or official results. Always verify official information with the relevant official source.

How to Read a Carta Ramalan 4D Chart

GDL Perdana

GDL Perdana is published every day. Each dated page shows that day's complete four-by-four number layout.

Ramalan 4D MTP & SGP

MTP and SGP charts are published on Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday. The date and category are shown together on every page.

Reading the Grid

Check the date and category first, then review all sixteen displayed positions. Highlighted cells are visual markers within that day's layout, not guaranteed numbers.

Using the Archive

Every published date has its own permanent page. Use the date shown on each page as the reference point when comparing charts across different days.
