How to Read a Carta Ramalan 4D Chart

What is Carta Ramalan 4D?

Carta Ramalan 4D is used here as a dated chart format for organizing four-digit chart information. Carta Lotto separates entries by date and category so that a visitor can identify the exact chart being viewed.

What does the GDL Perdana chart show?

A GDL Perdana entry contains a four-by-four layout with sixteen displayed positions. The date above the chart identifies the specific archive entry. The grid should be read as the complete layout rather than as a single four-digit result.

What are the MTP and SGP charts?

Ramalan 4D MTP & SGP entries are grouped as a separate chart category in the archive. Their dated pages use the same basic four-by-four presentation while keeping the MTP and SGP category clearly identified.

How should the 4 × 4 grid be read?

Start with the chart category and exact date. Then read the sixteen positions from left to right and top to bottom. The displayed digits are part of that specific chart layout.

What do highlighted positions mean?

Highlighted cells are visual markers used within the displayed chart. They are not official results and are not presented by Carta Lotto as guaranteed winning numbers.

How does the dated archive work?

Each published entry has its own permanent dated HTML page. Selecting a chart from the homepage opens that exact page. Previous and next navigation on individual pages can then be used to move through the same category's dated archive.

Chart information versus official results

A chart page and an official lottery result are different types of information. Carta Lotto's dated chart pages are informational references. Official results should always be checked through the appropriate official source.
