How Carta Lotto Organizes Daily Charts

Every chart is generated as a separate dated page. The date is included in the page filename, title, chart and structured data. Publishing a new chart therefore creates a new page instead of changing an older page.

GDL Perdana follows a daily schedule from Monday through Sunday. Ramalan 4D MTP & SGP follows its Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday schedule. When both categories have an entry on the same date, both remain separate pages.

The homepage automatically calculates the newest scheduled dates and places the newest available entries at the top. Previous dated pages remain available through their original files and URLs.
