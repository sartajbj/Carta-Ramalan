Carta Lotto & Carta Ramalan 4D Guide

Carta Lotto presents dated chart layouts in a simple format so visitors can identify the latest GDL Perdana update and the scheduled MTP and SGP entries without searching through older posts.

Every chart has its own date, chart type and sixteen-position grid. Highlighted cells are presentation markers within the published layout. They should not be treated as guaranteed future results.

GDL Perdana Daily Chart

GDL Perdana entries follow a daily update cycle. The newest dated entry appears first on the homepage, while the dedicated date URL allows a visitor to review a particular chart separately.

Ramalan 4D MTP and SGP Schedule

MTP and SGP entries are displayed on Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday in the current Carta Lotto schedule. These entries use a separate chart presentation to make the scheduled updates easy to identify.

How to Read a Carta Chart

Each layout contains sixteen positions arranged in four rows and four columns. Check the date and chart type first, then review the complete grid. Highlighted positions can be compared with the surrounding cells as part of the displayed chart structure.

Understanding Chart Observations

Some dated pages include simple observations such as repeated digits, even and odd counts, row totals and column totals. These are calculated from the displayed grid itself. They describe the chart layout and are not predictions of an official result.

Comparing Different Dates

When reviewing several entries, use the individual date shown on each page. A previous layout should remain separate from a newer update. This makes the chart archive easier to understand and reduces confusion between different dated entries.

Responsible Information

Carta Lotto provides chart information only. It does not sell lottery tickets, promise winning numbers or claim certainty about future results. Official results should always be obtained from the relevant official source.

Safety note: Never send money, passwords, banking information or other sensitive information to anyone claiming to provide secret or guaranteed numbers on behalf of Carta Lotto.
