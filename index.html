About Carta Lotto

Carta Lotto is an independent informational website that organizes dated Carta Ramalan 4D number charts. Two categories are published here: GDL Perdana, which is updated every day, and Ramalan 4D MTP & SGP, which follows a fixed weekly schedule of Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday. Every chart is kept on its own permanent, dated page so a specific day's layout can always be found and referenced later.

Carta Lotto is not an official lottery operator, does not sell tickets or betting slips, and does not accept payments in exchange for numbers. Nothing on this site should be understood as a guaranteed winning number or an official draw result. The charts shown here are Carta Lotto's own dated number layouts, presented for informational and archival purposes.

Important: Carta Lotto does not guarantee winning numbers, official results, or any future outcome. Always verify official draw information directly with the relevant official operator.

How to Read a Carta Ramalan 4D Chart

Every Carta Ramalan 4D chart on this site follows the same four-by-four layout: sixteen numbered positions arranged in four rows and four columns. Reading a chart is straightforward once you know what each part represents.

GDL Perdana

GDL Perdana is published every day of the week, Monday through Sunday. Each dated page shows that day's complete number layout, so the archive grows by one new GDL Perdana entry daily.

Ramalan 4D MTP & SGP

MTP and SGP charts follow a fixed schedule: Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday. On those three days, both a GDL Perdana entry and an MTP/SGP entry are available; on the remaining four days, only GDL Perdana is published.

Reading the Grid

Start by checking the date and category shown at the top of the chart, since these identify exactly which entry you're looking at. Then review the complete sixteen-position grid rather than focusing on a single cell. A handful of positions are visually highlighted on each chart — these are presentation markers within that specific layout, not predictions and not guaranteed numbers.

Official Results vs. Chart Information

It's worth being clear about the difference between what Carta Lotto publishes and an official lottery result. An official result comes directly from the operator running that draw. The charts on this site are Carta Lotto's own dated, organized layouts — useful as a reference and archive, but not a substitute for checking an operator's official results page.

Using the Dated Archive

Every published date has its own permanent page, so older charts remain available even as new ones are added. Each archive page includes Previous, Home and Next controls, making it easy to move between consecutive dates without losing track of which entry you started from.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Carta Ramalan 4D?

Carta Ramalan 4D is a dated chart format used to organize 4D number layouts by category and publishing date. Carta Lotto presents both GDL Perdana and Ramalan 4D MTP/SGP in this format.

How often is GDL Perdana updated?

Daily. A new dated GDL Perdana page is published every day of the week.

When is Ramalan 4D MTP/SGP published?

Every Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday. It is not published on the other four days of the week.

Are the highlighted numbers guaranteed to win?

No. Highlighted positions are visual markers on Carta Lotto's own published chart layout. They are not official results and are not guaranteed outcomes.

Is Carta Lotto an official lottery website?

No. Carta Lotto is an independent informational website and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any official lottery operator.

Where can I find older charts?

Every published date has its own permanent archive page. Use the Previous and Next controls on any chart page to browse other dates.
