About Carta Lotto

Carta Lotto organizes dated Carta Ramalan 4D chart pages into a simple archive. Each published entry keeps its own category, date and permanent page so older material remains easy to locate after newer updates are added.

GDL Perdana and MTP & SGP entries are kept separate because they follow different publishing schedules. The homepage combines the available pages in date order so visitors can browse the newest published archive entries without losing access to older dates.

Carta Lotto is an independent informational website. It does not operate an official lottery, sell tickets or publish official draw results.

Important: Charts on this website are maintained as dated informational references. Highlighted cells are visual markers in the published layouts and should not be confused with official results.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Carta Ramalan 4D on this website?

It refers to the dated chart archive format used on Carta Lotto for GDL Perdana and MTP & SGP entries.

Why does each chart have a separate page?

Keeping a permanent dated URL prevents older entries from being overwritten whenever another archive page is published.

Does the homepage chart match the full post?

Yes. The homepage loads the chart directly from the existing permanent HTML post instead of independently generating another grid.

What happens if a listed HTML post does not exist?

That entry is skipped automatically, so visitors are not shown a homepage card with a broken Read Full Post link.

Is Carta Lotto an official lottery operator?

No. Carta Lotto is an independent informational archive and is not an official lottery operator.
