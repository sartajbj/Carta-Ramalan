Carta Lotto & Carta Ramalan 4D

Welcome to Carta Lotto, a free destination for visitors looking for dated Carta Ramalan 4D and Carta 4D forecast chart information. The homepage is organized around the latest available date so returning visitors can quickly identify a new GDL Perdana chart and the scheduled MTP and SGP updates.

Every chart uses a clear 4×4 matrix. Four positions are highlighted to make the displayed pattern easier to inspect. GDL Perdana charts use a light red treatment, while Ramalan 4D MTP and SGP charts use a yellow/gold treatment.

GDL Perdana Daily Chart

The GDL Perdana section follows a seven-day cycle from Monday through Sunday. A dated chart is generated automatically from the current calendar date, allowing the newest daily information to appear before older updates.

Ramalan 4D MTP & SGP

MTP and SGP information is scheduled for Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday. On those days the additional chart appears together with the regular GDL Perdana update.

Carta Lotto VIP, Carta Planbee and Carta Senja

People searching for Carta Lotto VIP, Carta Planbee or Carta Senja may be looking for different chart formats and forecast information. Carta Lotto keeps its presentation focused on dated, clearly labeled chart information rather than claiming that a particular number is certain to win.

How to Read a Carta 4D Chart

Start by checking the date shown on the chart. Then review all sixteen positions in the 4×4 matrix. The highlighted positions are visual markers within the forecast chart; they should not be interpreted as a guarantee of a future lottery result.
